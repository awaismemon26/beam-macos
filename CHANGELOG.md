## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21

The first release.

### Added

- **⌃ Tab window switcher** for the frontmost app: MRU order, across Desktops, restores minimized windows;
  release Control to jump, Esc to cancel.
- **⌥ Tab window search** across every app and Desktop, by app name (with nicknames such as `vscode`) or
  window title.
- **⌥ Space launcher** with fuzzy, usage-aware app search and Beam commands (Settings, Switch Windows).
- **Calculator** powered by SoulverCore: percentages, arithmetic, units, currencies (live ECB rates), time
  zones and dates. Enter copies the answer.
- **Settings**: Open at Login, Show in Menu Bar, rebindable Beam Hotkey, Follow System Appearance or fixed
  light/dark, and an About pane.
- Guided Accessibility onboarding, including a one-click reset for stale permission entries.
- Universal build (Apple silicon and Intel) for macOS 15 and later.
